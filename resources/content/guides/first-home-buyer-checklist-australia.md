# First Home Buyer Checklist: Documents You Need

Preparing documents early helps first home buyers move faster when the right property appears. Lenders assess your identity, income, savings, and existing commitments.

## Identity and personal details

- Photo ID (driver licence or passport)
- Medicare card or secondary ID where required
- Current address history

## Income evidence

**PAYG employees:** Recent payslips (usually 2), employment letter, and last tax return if requested.

**Self-employed:** Two years tax returns, financial statements, and BAS/statements may be required.

## Savings and deposit

- Bank statements showing deposit funds (often 3–6 months)
- Evidence of genuine savings or gift/deposit source declarations
- Statements for other assets (shares, super if applicable)

## Liabilities

- Credit card limits (not just balances)
- Personal loans, car loans, HECS/HELP
- Buy-now-pay-later accounts

## Property details (once you find a home)

- Contract of sale
- Rental estimates if applicable

## Tip

Pre-approval before you bid or offer gives you a clearer budget and stronger confidence at inspections.
