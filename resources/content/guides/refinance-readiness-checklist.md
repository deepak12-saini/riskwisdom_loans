# Refinance Readiness Checklist for Australian Homeowners

Before you refinance, work through these eight questions.

1. **What is my current rate and comparison rate?**
2. **Are there exit or break fees on my existing loan?**
3. **How much equity do I have?** (Recent comparable sales help.)
4. **What features do I use?** (Offset, redraw, split.)
5. **What is my goal?** Lower repayments, faster payoff, cash-out, or consolidation.
6. **How long will I keep this loan?**
7. **Is my employment or income changing soon?**
8. **Have I compared at least 3 lender options?**

## When to wait

If break costs outweigh savings, or you plan to sell soon, refinancing may not be worthwhile. A broker can quantify the break-even point.
