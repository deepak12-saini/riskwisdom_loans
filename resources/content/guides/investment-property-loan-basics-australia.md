# Investment Property Loan Basics for Australian Investors

Investment lending is assessed differently from owner-occupier loans. Understanding the basics helps you plan portfolio growth with fewer surprises.

## Key differences

- Deposit requirements are often higher (commonly 10–20%)
- Interest rates may differ from owner-occupier products
- Rental income is considered but usually discounted in servicing tests

## Interest-only vs principal & interest

Interest-only periods can improve cash flow but do not build equity through repayments. Lenders may limit interest-only terms based on policy.

## Equity strategies

Equity in existing property can fund deposits for further investments. Lenders assess total exposure across your portfolio.

## Structure matters

Loan structure affects tax outcomes — speak with your accountant alongside your broker for coordinated advice.
