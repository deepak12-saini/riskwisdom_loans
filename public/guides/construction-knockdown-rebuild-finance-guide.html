Construction & Knockdown-Rebuild Finance Guide

A simple overview of how construction lending works, what changes during progress payments, and the questions worth answering before you sign a building contract.

What this guide covers

  • How construction finance differs from a standard purchase loan
  • Progress payment stages and lender release timing
  • Buffers for cost overruns, variations, and timing delays
  • Equity, deposit, and valuation considerations
  • Planning for interest-only periods during the build

Key reminders

  • Building contracts, council approvals, and valuations all affect timing.
  • Knockdown-rebuild structures can be assessed differently from a straight construction loan.
  • Cash-flow planning matters because progress payments may not line up neatly with builder expectations.
