First Home Buyer's Guide

A practical starting guide for Australian first home buyers who want more clarity before they make an offer.

What this guide covers

  • Deposit expectations and how lenders assess genuine savings
  • Pre-approval, what it means, and when to organise it
  • Common documents to prepare before applying
  • Government schemes and grant conversations to ask about
  • What happens from application through to settlement

Before you start inspecting properties

Know your budget range, expected cash contribution, and likely monthly repayment comfort zone. Online calculators are useful, but lender policy and living expenses often change the real borrowing number.

Questions worth asking early

  • How much deposit do I need for my target purchase?
  • Am I likely to need lenders mortgage insurance?
  • What documents will the lender ask for first?
  • Should I seek pre-approval before making offers?
