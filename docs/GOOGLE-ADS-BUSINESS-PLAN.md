## Executive summary

Riskwisdom Loans can run **Google Search Ads** to generate enquiries from people actively searching for home loans, refinancing, and broker services.

**Recommended start:**

| Item | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| First campaign | Rate review / refinance |
| Landing page | `/enquire/refinance` (conversion landing) |
| Daily budget | **A$20–25/day** (~A$600–750/month) |
| Test period | 2 weeks, then review before scaling |
| Expected cost per lead | A$60–100 (estimate; varies by market) |

Leads are captured on the website and visible in **Admin → Enquiries** (filter: **Paid ads**).

---

## 1. Business goal

**Primary goal:** Generate qualified enquiries (rate review forms + phone calls).

**Success looks like:**

1. Person clicks ad on Google  
2. Lands on a focused page (e.g. rate review)  
3. Submits form or calls  
4. Lead appears in `/admin/enquiries`  
5. Broker calls back within 15 minutes (business hours)  
6. Application lodged → settlement → revenue  

**We are not optimising for:** page views, brand awareness only, or traffic without lead capture.

---

## 2. Strategy (first 6 weeks)

### Phase 1 — Test (Weeks 1–2)

- Launch **one** Search campaign: **Rate Review / Refinance**
- Budget: **A$20–25/day**
- Landing page: `https://riskwisdomloans.com.au/enquire/refinance`
- Track leads in admin with **Paid ads** filter
- Review: spend, clicks, leads, cost per lead

### Phase 2 — Optimise (Weeks 3–4)

- Add **negative keywords** (jobs, careers, free downloads)
- Pause keywords that spend with zero leads
- Refine ad headlines based on **Search terms** report in Google Ads
- Confirm conversion tracking (`generate_lead` via GA4/GTM)

### Phase 3 — Scale (Weeks 5–6)

Only if cost per lead is acceptable:

- Add **First home buyer** campaign → `/first-home-buyer`
- Add **Refinance rates** campaign → `/refinance-home-loan-rates`
- Increase daily budget gradually (e.g. A$30–40/day total)

### What we will not do in Week 1

- Send all traffic to the homepage only  
- Run Display Network or video brand campaigns  
- Increase budget before reviewing 2 weeks of data  
- Ignore lead follow-up speed

### Starting budget options

| Option | Per day | Per week | Per month (approx.) | Use when |
|--------|---------|----------|---------------------|----------|
| **Conservative** | A$20 | ~A$140 | ~A$600 | Minimum risk test |

### Estimated performance (conservative — not guaranteed)

| Metric | Month 1 estimate |
|--------|------------------|
| Ad spend | A$600–750 |
| Clicks | 80–120 |
| Website leads | 6–12 |
| Cost per lead | A$60–100 |
| Enquiries per week | ~1.5–3 |

**Example:** A$600 spend ÷ 8 leads = **A$75 per lead**

### ROI logic

- If **2–3 leads per month** become serious applications → ads likely worthwhile  
- If **0–1 leads** after 2 weeks → pause, review keywords/landing page, fix tracking  
- Broker income per settlement vs cost per lead determines long-term scale

## 11. Operational requirements

For ads to be profitable, **operations matter as much as the website:**

1. **Call leads within 15 minutes** during business hours  
2. **Check admin enquiries** morning, lunch, end of day  
3. **After-hours chat** leads followed up next business morning  
4. **Weekly review** of Google Ads search terms + negatives  
5. **Do not scale budget** without reviewing cost per lead
